EBITDA = revenue − variable − per-event production − fixed overhead (incl. payroll, end-of-service, staff insurance & air tickets). Depreciation is CapEx straight-lined over its useful life (set on the Setup tab), starting when operations begin — a non-cash charge, so EBITDA − depreciation = EBIT. The Cash Flow tab is unaffected: it shows the actual CapEx outlay when spent, not depreciation (including both would double-count). Calendar years 2026 and the final year are partial.

Simple payback = total CapEx ÷ net contribution per event (steady-state, no growth). Cash payback (incl. pre-op) is when cumulative cash — after paying CapEx and pre-operation overhead — returns to zero. Balance Sheet

Year-end position. Assets (cash + fixed assets net of depreciation) always equal Liabilities + Equity (paid-in capital + retained earnings). All figures AED.

Annual balance sheet — year-end —
Monthly balance sheet

Cash = the cumulative cash position from the Cash Flow tab. Net fixed assets = cumulative CapEx − accumulated depreciation (each asset over its own useful life). Deferred ticket revenue is cash collected for events not yet held (registrations arrive the "ticket collection lead" months ahead), released to revenue when the event runs. End-of-service provision is the accrued UAE gratuity — expensed in the P&L but held as a cash reserve, not paid until an employee leaves. Retained earnings = cumulative profit after depreciation (EBIT); paid-in capital = opening funding you inject. Assets = liabilities + equity to the dirham every period. Negative equity means cumulative losses exceed capital injected — that gap is the funding still required.
