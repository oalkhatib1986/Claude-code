Monthly cash flow statement

Cash basis: ticket revenue collected in the event month. CapEx spread across pre-op months (toggle on Assumptions). Overhead paid every month including pre-op. Net cash = operating P&L − CapEx. Peak funding requirement is the deepest cumulative trough (excludes any opening funding you inject).

Payback Analysis

How long the one-time capital takes to come back — both as a simple per-event calculation and as the real cash payback including the pre-operation burn. All figures AED.

Simple payback = total CapEx ÷ net contribution per event (steady-state, no growth). Cash payback (incl. pre-op) is when cumulative cash — after paying CapEx and pre-operation overhead — returns to zero. Break-even athletes is the attendance per event at which an event exactly covers its production + allocated overhead.
