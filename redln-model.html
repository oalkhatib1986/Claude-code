Monthly income statement

EBITDA = revenue − variable − per-event production − fixed overhead (incl. payroll, end-of-service, staff insurance & air tickets). Depreciation is CapEx straight-lined over its useful life (set on the Setup tab), starting when operations begin — a non-cash charge, so EBITDA − depreciation = EBIT. The Cash Flow tab is unaffected: it shows the actual CapEx outlay when spent, not depreciation (including both would double-count). Calendar years 2026 and the final year are partial.

Unit payback & capital

Direct costs: Events carry per-athlete variable, per-event production and prizes; Group Classes carry trainers and consumables; F&B and Retail are net commission/margin (no direct cost). Overhead (rent, payroll, utilities, marketing, …) and depreciation are shared costs allocated to each unit by its share of revenue. Unit operating profit sums to the company EBIT. Simple payback = each unit's revenue-share of CapEx ÷ its annual operating profit — a rule-of-thumb; the real, whole-company recovery date is the cash breakeven on the Dashboard and Payback tabs.

Cumulative operating cash vs. capital0 line = capital fully recovered

These two answer different questions and won't match. CapEx payback (rule of thumb) = total CapEx ÷ the net contribution a single event leaves after its allocated overhead — a capital-intensity ratio that ignores the pre-operation period and excludes group-class income, so it reads slower. Cash payback (real) is the actual date cumulative cash returns to zero after the deepest dip, counting everything: pre-op burn, CapEx, every event, group classes and ticket-cash timing. The cash date is the one to trust; the rule of thumb is just a sanity check on how hard the CapEx works. Break-even athletes is the attendance per event at which an event exactly covers its production + allocated overhead.

Balance Sheet

Year-end position. Assets (cash + fixed assets net of depreciation) always equal Liabilities + Equity (paid-in capital + retained earnings). All figures AED.

Pre-opening balance sheet —

The financial position at two points before trading begins: at inception (capital injected, nothing spent yet) and at the start of operations (end of the pre-operation period — after buying CapEx and absorbing pre-opening overhead, and collecting any early ticket cash). This is the balance sheet the business carries into its first event.

Annual balance sheet — year-end —
Monthly balance sheet

Cash = the cumulative cash position from the Cash Flow tab. Net fixed assets = cumulative CapEx − accumulated depreciation (each asset over its own useful life). Deferred ticket revenue is cash collected for events not yet held (registrations arrive the "ticket collection lead" months ahead), released to revenue when the event runs. End-of-service provision is the accrued UAE gratuity — expensed in the P&L but held as a cash reserve, not paid until an employee leaves. Retained earnings = cumulative profit after depreciation (EBIT); paid-in capital = opening funding you inject. Assets = liabilities + equity to the dirham every period. Negative equity means cumulative losses exceed capital injected — that gap is the funding still required.
