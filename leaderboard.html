How it works on real hardware

  1. Mount a cheap Android tablet on each rower / SkiErg. It pairs to the PM5 over Bluetooth — no wires, always on.
  2. Athlete taps their crew, hits start. The tablet reads distance · split · watts · rate straight off the monitor.
  3. Every tablet pushes to one shared board. This screen re-sorts live and calls the finish.
