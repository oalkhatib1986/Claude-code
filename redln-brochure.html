01Executive Overview

A gym you don't just join.
One you compete in.

REDLN is a purpose-built venue for competitive fitness — a permanent home for the Hyrox-style team-race format that has turned amateur athletes into a global movement. Under one roof we combine a competition arena, coached boutique training, a calendar of live ticketed events, a performance retail store and a fuel café.

Boutique studios sell the workout. Commercial gyms sell access. Neither sells the thing people actually want to be part of — the competition, the leaderboard, the team, the belonging. REDLN makes competition the product, and makes it available every single day. This document sets out the concept, the category we intend to create, our values, and the financial plan for the first venue.

To launch Season 01 in Dubai, REDLN requires AED 1.73M in capital — AED 0.80M of fit-out and equipment plus AED 0.94M of pre-opening runway. The venue is EBITDA-positive by Year 1 and net-profit positive by Year 1 on this base case, built on four diversified, largely recurring revenue streams.
02The New Category

Competitive Fitness.

Hyrox proved there is enormous, unmet demand for amateur competition — hundreds of thousands of everyday athletes paying to race, train for a result, and belong to a scene. But that demand is served by events: itinerant, a few times a year, in rented convention halls. Between races, competitors go back to ordinary gyms that were never built for them.

REDLN closes that gap. We take the competition format and give it a permanent home — a venue engineered around the race, with live scoring, seasons, leaderboards, coached preparation and a spectator floor. We are not opening another boutique studio or another commercial gym. We are defining a new category — Competitive Fitness — train, compete, spectate and belong, year-round, in one place. First-mover advantage in a category nobody else occupies yet.

03The Concept

Four businesses.
One arena.

REDLN earns from four revenue streams under one roof — the exact four in the financial model. They share one competition arena and one community, so each stream feeds the others: events bring people in, training keeps them, and the café and store monetise the time they already spend on site.

01 · Events

Competition Events

Ticketed team-race competitions on the arena floor, with spectators and sponsors. Our race format RUN·BIKE·ROW·SKI — the brand and acquisition engine, and the reason the category exists.

02 · Training

Group Training

Coached boutique classes that prepare members for the race format — programmed in seasons, ranked on the leaderboard. The largest, most recurring revenue stream.

03 · F&B

The Café

Coffee, shakes and recovery — a reason to arrive early and stay late. A high-margin, everyday stream that turns dwell time into income.

04 · Retail

The Store

Performance apparel and gear that lets members wear the badge outside the venue. A margin stream and a walking brand billboard.

The Glue · Not a revenue line

The Arena · Membership · Seasons

The permanent competition floor with live scoring, seasons and leaderboards is the stage all four businesses share — and the reason they stick. Members join teams; teams compete across seasons. You don't quit mid-season, and you don't quit on your team.

04The Product · How It Works

One workout.
Run. Ski. Bike. Row.

Every REDLN race is the same fixed, erg-based workout — Run, Ski, Bike, Row, no skilled movements, all on the machines. Enter solo or as a team of four. Two divisions run the identical format at a different load — Everyday (scaled) and Elite (full). The clock decides it: lowest total time wins, and every entry is chip-timed.

The season workout is published two to four weeks out and never changes, so every race is a direct shot at a personal best. Four monthly races make a season; two seasons a year, and Season 02 brings a brand-new workout.

06Add-ons & Engagement

More ways in.
More to spend on.

Beyond the entry fee, REDLN monetises intent — the athletes who want an edge pay for it. Every add-on is built into the platform and priced with VAT included.

Add-on · AED 50

Pick your heat

Heats are assigned at random 48 hours out, free. Want to choose your slot? AED 50 — captain only, for the whole team — changeable to any open slot up to 48h before.

Add-on · AED 50 / athlete

Trial Day

The day before the race, book a private one-hour lane on the real track to rehearse the season workout — solo or with the team.

Zero-effort demand capture

Waitlists

Sold-out categories keep a waitlist. The moment a spot opens, the next athlete’s saved card is charged automatically and the entry is confirmed.

Why it matters: heats, Trial Day and waitlists convert enthusiasm into incremental, high-margin revenue — and a waitlist means a sold-out race keeps selling itself with no staff effort.

08Governance · The Rule Book

Every result,
earned.

A published rule book governs the whole competition — the same one members read on the site. It is what makes the leaderboard credible and the category defensible. The essentials:

Eligibility

Who can race

18 and over. Solo or a team of four — all-men, all-women or mixed. You can race in more than one team per race, provided no two teams field the same four and heats don’t clash.

Format & scoring

The clock decides

Four ergs — Run, Ski, Bike, Row. Lowest total time wins, every entry chip-timed, splits live on screen. The season workout is fixed once published.

Teams

Captain & locking

The buyer is captain and the only one who invites; athletes accept to join. Teams edit until 48h out, then lock; a completed team locks for good, so times stay comparable. Names are permanent.

Entries

Passes & transfers

Single tickets or a four-race season pass. Non-refundable, but transferable up to a week before. Sold out? Join the waitlist — first in line, auto-charged.

Fair play

Code of conduct

Full distance on every machine, in order. No erg or chip tampering, no proxies, no PEDs. Breaches mean penalties, disqualification or a ban. Officials’ call is final.

Payments & data

Secure by default

All prices include 5% VAT. Cards are encrypted and tokenised by a PCI-DSS provider — REDLN never sees or stores full card numbers or CVC.

The full rule book — season & workout, format, eligibility, entries & transfers, teams, heat times, Trial Day, waitlists, payments, code of conduct, results & disputes and safety — is published in full on the member site.

11Values & Ethics

What we stand for.

Competition with integrity

Every result is earned, measured and transparent. Fair heats, honest scoring, clean sport. The leaderboard is sacred.

Everyone on the start line

From first-timer to elite — everyday and elite divisions side by side. The category is inclusive by design; the arena welcomes all levels.

Team before ego

We are built around teams, not individuals. You show up because people are counting on you. Belonging is the product.

Measured progress

What gets measured gets better. Seasons, splits and personal bests give members a reason to return and a story to tell.

Clean fuel

Real food and honest nutrition. The café serves performance, not marketing — quality coffee, transparent shakes, proper recovery.

Responsible operations

Efficient, durable fit-out; long-life equipment; sensible energy use. We build a venue meant to last a decade, not a season.

Future upside

The four streams are just the base case.

The base-case financials count only what launches on day one. Each of these is additive on the same fixed cost of the venue, arena and platform — so most of the revenue drops through. They are the levers that turn a profitable venue into a category business.

+ Sponsorship

Sponsorship & media

Floor, LED screen, heat and kit sponsorship, plus broadcast and streaming inventory powered by Redline CORE™ as the audience scales.

+ Licensing

Franchise & licensing

License the format, the brand and the Redline CORE™ operating system to new operators and cities once the playbook is proven.

+ Corporate

Corporate & team events

Private race days, company leagues and team-building bookings on the arena floor — high-ticket, midweek, and filling otherwise-quiet daytime capacity.

+ Calendar

More race days

Extra events, additional seasons and special formats beyond the four-race calendar — more entries against the same fit-out.

+ Classes

Expanded class schedule

More coached slots per day and new formats as membership grows — scaling the largest, most recurring stream with demand.

+ Rental

Partner & space rental

Rent the floor, lanes and space to brands, coaches and pop-up activations between events — partner activation and rental fees on idle hours.

Revenue is recognised net of 5% VAT. The café and retail are modelled as standalone monthly business units, independent of the event calendar. The future streams above are not in the base-case projections — they are upside on top of the plan.

14Payback & Returns

A hard asset
with a moat.

REDLN is a capital-intensive, physical, brand-owning business — a real venue, a real community, and the software platform that runs both. The reward for that capital is a durable asset and ownership of an entire category. Payback reflects that profile.

Capital Payback

~2.1 years

Cumulative operating cash recovers the full AED 1.73M injection (projected on the same growth assumptions).

Founder Payback

~3.1 years

The founder funds 100% of the capital but owns 70% after a 30% sweat-equity grant, so dividends return the injection a little later.

Distribution Policy

100k floor sweep

The business keeps AED 100,000 in the bank at each period-end; all excess cash is distributed to shareholders by ownership (70 / 30).

The base case holds every price flat in real terms. The levers that most improve payback — class price, class volume, event frequency, sponsorship and café throughput — are all live in the model. Tune them and the picture moves quickly.
