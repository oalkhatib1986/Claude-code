01Executive Overview

A gym you don't
just join. One you
compete in.

REDLN is a purpose-built venue for competitive fitness — a permanent home for the Hyrox-style team-race format that has turned amateur athletes into a global movement. Under one roof we combine a competition arena, coached boutique training, a calendar of live ticketed events, a performance retail store and a fuel café.

Boutique studios sell the workout. Commercial gyms sell access. Neither sells the thing people actually want to be part of — the competition, the leaderboard, the team, the belonging. REDLN makes competition the product, and makes it available every single day. This document sets out the concept, the category we intend to create, our values, and the financial plan for the first venue.

To launch Season 01 in Dubai, REDLN requires AED 2.15M in capital — AED 1.59M of fit-out and equipment plus AED 0.47M of pre-opening runway. The venue is EBITDA-positive by Year 2 and net-profit positive by Year 5 on this base case, built on four diversified, largely recurring revenue streams.
02The New Category

Competition-Driven Fitness.

Hyrox proved there is enormous, unmet demand for amateur competition — hundreds of thousands of everyday athletes paying to race, train for a result, and belong to a scene. But that demand is served by events: itinerant, a few times a year, in rented convention halls. Between races, competitors go back to ordinary gyms that were never built for them.

REDLN closes that gap. We take the competition format and give it a permanent home — a venue engineered around the race, with live scoring, seasons, leaderboards, coached preparation and a spectator floor. We are not opening another boutique studio or another commercial gym. We are defining a new category: the Competition-Driven Fitness Center — train, compete, spectate and belong, year-round, in one place. First-mover advantage in a category nobody else occupies yet.

03The Concept

Five businesses,
one venue.

Each element reinforces the others: the arena and events build the brand and bring people in; the coached studio converts them into recurring members; retail and café monetise the time they already spend on site and deepen the identity.

The Arena

Compete

A full competition floor — rows of rowers, skis, bikes and functional stations, live scoring and leaderboards. The stage for our race format, RUN·BIKE·ROW·SKI, and the reason the category exists.

The Studio

Train

Coached boutique group classes that prepare members for the race format — programmed in seasons, ranked on the leaderboard, led by a roster of head and assistant coaches.

The Events

Belong

A recurring calendar of ticketed team-race competitions with spectators and sponsors. The brand engine — acquisition, content, and community, all in one night.

The Store

Fuel the identity

A performance retail store — apparel and gear that lets members wear the badge outside the venue. A revenue stream and a brand billboard.

The Café

Fuel the body

Coffee, shakes and recovery — a reason to arrive early and stay late, and a high-margin, everyday revenue stream that turns dwell time into income.

The System

Membership · Teams · Seasons

Members join teams; teams compete across seasons; every result feeds a live leaderboard. Competition is what makes membership sticky — you don't quit mid-season, and you don't quit on your team.

05Values & Ethics

What we stand for.

Competition with integrity

Every result is earned, measured and transparent. Fair heats, honest scoring, clean sport. The leaderboard is sacred.

Everyone on the start line

From first-timer to elite — everyday and elite divisions side by side. The category is inclusive by design; the arena welcomes all levels.

Team before ego

We are built around teams, not individuals. You show up because people are counting on you. Belonging is the product.

Measured progress

What gets measured gets better. Seasons, splits and personal bests give members a reason to return and a story to tell.

Clean fuel

Real food and honest nutrition. The café serves performance, not marketing — quality coffee, transparent shakes, proper recovery.

Responsible operations

Efficient, durable fit-out; long-life equipment; sensible energy use. We build a venue meant to last a decade, not a season.

06Business Model

Four streams.
One roof.

Diversification is the point. The recurring studio and café smooth the cash line; events and retail drive the brand and the margin. No single stream carries the venue.

01

Group Training

Coached boutique classes — the recurring revenue engine and largest single stream.

02

Events & Tickets

Ticketed team-race competitions — the acquisition and brand engine, plus sponsorship upside.

03

Café / F&B

Everyday, high-margin fuel that monetises dwell time. A standalone unit.

04

Retail

Performance apparel and gear — a margin stream and a walking brand billboard.

+

Sponsorship

Future upside — brand partners on the event floor, screens and kit as the audience scales.

Revenue is recognised net of 5% VAT. The café and retail are modelled as standalone monthly business units, independent of the event calendar.

08Payback & Returns

A venue,
not an app.

REDLN is a capital-intensive, physical, brand-owning business — closer to a hospitality venue than a software play. The reward for that capital is a durable location, a real community, and ownership of an entire category. Payback reflects that profile.

Capital Payback

~6.6 years

Cumulative operating cash recovers the full AED 2.15M injection (projected on the same growth assumptions).

Founder Payback

~7.5 years

The founder funds 100% of the capital but owns 70% after a 30% sweat-equity grant, so dividends return the injection a little later.

Distribution Policy

100k floor sweep

The business keeps AED 100,000 in the bank at each period-end; all excess cash is distributed to shareholders by ownership (70 / 30).

The base case is deliberately conservative and holds every price flat in real terms. The levers that most improve payback — class price, class volume, event frequency, sponsorship and café throughput — are all live in the model. Tune them and the picture moves quickly.
