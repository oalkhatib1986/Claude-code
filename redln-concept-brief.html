01 — The concept REDLN.

A permanent home for competitive fitness.

Group fitness proved there is enormous, unmet demand for training that feels like sport — teams, seasons, a clock that matters and a crowd that cares. REDLN gives that energy a permanent address: part gym, part arena, part clubhouse. Members don’t just work out. They join a team, train for a season, and race under the lights.

01

The Club

A membership, not a drop-in. Members belong to a team and train year-round in a purpose-built strength-and-conditioning space designed around the race format — coached, programmed, and built for people who want a goal, not just a gym.

02

The Format

Structured, repeatable team races — the same discipline that has become a global movement. Programmed in seasons, ranked on a live leaderboard. You don’t quit mid-season; you climb it. Race solo or as a team.

03

Race Day

The peak of the season runs as a live event on the arena floor — a DJ, lights, haze, a spectator zone and a leaderboard that updates the instant a time posts. It looks and feels like a sport, because it is one.

04

The Community

The leaderboard, the team, the belonging. Members wear the badge outside the venue and bring the next member in. It gives people a reason to return and a story to tell — the engine of a brand, not just a business.

02 — The experience & the space REDLN.

Built like a venue, run like a sport.

REDLN is designed to be felt the moment you walk in — raw, industrial and cinematic, closer to a concert floor than a fitness studio. That experience is exactly why the right building matters.

1

Arrive

An industrial warehouse transformed — exposed structure, moody light, a single bold red line running through it. You feel you’ve entered somewhere, not signed into a gym.

2

Train

Coached, seasonal programming on a purpose-built floor. Members prepare together, week after week, for a race that’s on the calendar.

3

Race

Parallel lanes framed by truss towers rigged with speakers and moving lights. A host on the mic, a live leaderboard, a spectator floor. The room turns into an event.

4

Belong

Recovery, F&B and a social corner keep members in the building — and bring the community back between race days.

What the space needs
  • Building typeIndustrial / warehouse character
  • Ceiling heightHigh clear height for lighting & truss rigging
  • StructureOpen, column-light clear span
  • FootprintA single large hall for lanes, training & spectators
  • PowerCapacity for AV, lighting & production
  • AccessLoad-in for events; visitor parking nearby
  • FrontagePresence for branding & athlete entry

Exact sizing is flexible — we’d shape the fit-out to the unit. The priorities are volume (height), openness (clear span) and character.
