FIG.01 "The Redline" Why we exist

One line.
Everyone meets it.

red·line — the point past which an engine can't hold. In your body: the second before you quit. Most training tiptoes around it; REDLN is built to take you straight to it. Every race and every class drives you to your redline, puts a number on it, and dares you past it. That's the name — and the whole idea: find your limit, then cross it. And we built it for everyone — a real stage where athletes of all levels can actually compete, open year-round, so there's always a next race to chase.

The season

Race as many
as you like.

Every race in a season runs the same fixed workout. Enter a single race, a couple, or the whole season — entirely up to you. Every race is a chance to beat your own best time. Race it more than once and you'll see it drop: come back, go faster, climb the leaderboard.

No season commitment

No commitment to the whole season. Do one race, a couple, or every one — your call, every time.

Improve every time

Same workout all season means your times are directly comparable — so each race back is a shot at a new personal best, and a chance to climb the leaderboard.

Two seasons a year — Season 02 (Sep–Dec) brings a brand-new workout, so each season is a fresh benchmark and a fresh leaderboard to climb.

The Why

Why we built REDLN.

Two reasons. One is the redline — the limit worth chasing. The other is you: everyone deserves a place to chase it, people to chase it with, and a stage to test it on. Amateur athletes rarely get to really compete — so we built them a home to do it in.

Mission
Make the limit visible

We turn an abstract feeling — redlining — into a measured, repeatable race. You can’t improve what you can’t see. We show you the line so you can move it.

The stage
A stage for amateurs

Most of us stop competing the day we leave school — there's simply nowhere to do it. But everyone who trains quietly wants to test it for real. REDLN gives everyday athletes that stage: a start line, a clock, a crowd and a rank. Competition built for amateurs, not just elites.

Home
A home, not a one-off

Amateur competition today is itinerant — a few times a year, in a rented hall. You race, you pack up, you wait months. REDLN is a permanent home: open year-round, a fresh season every few months, always here when you want it.

Community
Belong here

The race is the excuse; the community is the point. Teams that come back season after season, a leaderboard with your name on it, people who notice when you’re not there. You’re not a walk-in — you’re a regular, and this is your crew.

Effort
Effort over ego

Loads scale, the clock is honest. A first-timer and a national athlete run the same format and both leave having met their own line.

Progress
Progress, on the record

Every race is timed and logged. Progress isn’t a feeling — it’s a faster line than last time, in front of everyone. Come back and chase it.

Momentum
Always something to chase

There’s always a next race and a next season — a reason to keep training, and a team counting on you to show. Not a one-off high, but momentum you build on all year.

Origin
Built in Dubai

Born in the city that turned its own name into a verb — to Dubai-it is to do the extraordinary, with excellence, in record time. Homegrown, not franchised — and proud of it.

Promise
You’ll surprise yourself

Everyone underestimates their line. Everyone. The best part of every race is the face people make when they cross theirs.

FAQ

Before you race.

The things people ask most. Still stuck? The contact page has a human on the other end.

Who can race?

Anyone 18 or over. Race solo, or bring a team of four — all men, all women, or mixed. First-timer or national athlete, the line's the same.

Is REDLN only races, or can I train there too?

Both. REDLN is a Competitive Fitness Club: race the season, and train year-round in coached classes built on the race format. Come for one, come for both.

Is there a café and store on site?

Yes. Coffee, shakes and recovery at the café, REDLN apparel and gear in the store — so the badge lives on after you leave the floor.

Are there categories for different levels?

Two: Everyday and Elite — same format, scaled load. No skilled movements, it's all erg-based. Pick the one that fits where you're at.

Can people watch?

Please do. Spectators of all ages are welcome — live scoring on the big screen, and the crowd is half the point.

Is hydration provided?

Yes — hydration's on us for every athlete on race day.

How is it scored?

By the clock. Finish the workout in the lowest total time and you top the leaderboard. That simple.

Are there partners on the day?

Yes — partners across fitness, recovery, hydration and food are on site for every race.

What if I can't make it after registering?

Entries are non-refundable, but you can transfer your spot up to one week before the race.

Where do the races happen?

The DXB Race-haus — our custom-built arena in Dubai. Exact venue and directions land with your team before race day.

Is there parking?

Plenty, at and around the venue. Full directions and parking reach your team before race day.

How do I reach you?

Questions, partnerships or sponsorships — E: hello@redln.ae  M: +971 50 123 4567. We answer fast.
